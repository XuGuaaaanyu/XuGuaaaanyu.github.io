    {In-Hand Object Reorientation on a Dexterous Robotic Hand\projlinks{}{}}
    {\href{https://irmv.sjtu.edu.cn/}{\underline{The IRMV Lab}}, Shanghai Jiao Tong University}
    {May. 2026 - Sep. 2026}
    {Advisor: Prof. Hesheng Wang}
\vspace{2mm}
\resumeItemListStart
    \item \textbf{Led DexOrient}, a \textbf{proprioception-only} framework for \textbf{precise command-following in-hand reorientation} on a \textbf{16-DoF LEAP Hand}, with no vision or touch at deployment.
    \item Trained a \textbf{sim-to-real PPO policy in Isaac Lab} with domain randomization, reaching \textbf{100\% hardware success} given ground-truth rotation progress.
    \item Decoupled \textbf{rotation progress estimation} from \textbf{RMA-style object property adaptation}, raising hardware success from \textbf{44.0\% to 81.0\%}.
    \item Fine-tuned the estimator via \textbf{on-policy hardware DAgger} on its own closed-loop rollouts, raising success from \textbf{57.5\% to 81.0\%}.
    \item Robust to \textbf{unseen objects}, \textbf{mid-command mass changes}, and \textbf{external disturbances}.
